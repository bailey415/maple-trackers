MAPLE TRACKER - no install needed
=================================

Reads YOUR character's EXP and mesos off the screen while you play
MapleStory on GeForce NOW, and shows both on one live dashboard in your
browser.

HOW TO USE
  1. Double-click MapleTracker.exe. A black window opens and your
     browser shows the dashboard.
  2. Click the LV badge and type your level (only once). Click the name
     to type your character name.
  3. Open your inventory in game and leave it open to track mesos too.
  4. Keep the black window open while you play. Close it to stop.

  If the mesos side asks "Does your inventory say ...?", click Yes
  (or type the real number and click Fix).

IF WINDOWS SAYS "Windows protected your PC"
  This happens with small apps that aren't from a big company.
  Click "More info" -> "Run anyway".

TIPS
  - The game has to be visible on screen (not minimised or covered).
  - Your stats are saved in a "data" folder next to the app, so closing
    and reopening keeps today's totals.
