MAPLE TRACKERS - no install needed
==================================

Two little apps that read YOUR character's numbers off the screen while
you play MapleStory on GeForce NOW and show live stats in your browser.

EXP TRACKER  (folder "EXP Tracker")
  1. Double-click ExpTracker.exe. A black window opens and your browser
     shows the dashboard.
  2. Click the LV badge and type your level (only once). Click the name
     to type your character name.
  3. Play. Keep the black window open. Close it to stop.

MESOS TRACKER  (folder "Mesos Tracker")
  1. Open your inventory in game and leave it open on screen.
  2. Double-click MesosTracker.exe.
  3. If the dashboard asks "Does your inventory say ...?", click Yes
     (or type the real number and click Fix).

Both can run at the same time.

IF WINDOWS SAYS "Windows protected your PC"
  This happens with small apps that aren't from a big company.
  Click "More info" -> "Run anyway".

TIPS
  - The game has to be visible on screen (not minimised or covered).
  - Your stats are saved in a "data" folder next to each app, so closing
    and reopening keeps today's totals.
