MAPLE TRACKER (EXP + MESOS IN ONE)
==================================

One window, one dashboard: your EXP and your mesos side by side.

HOW TO USE
  1. Double-click run.bat. Your browser opens http://127.0.0.1:8765
  2. Play with your inventory open to track mesos as well as EXP.
  3. Leave the black window open. Close it to stop.

  Click the LV badge to set your level, click the name to rename.
  The "last 5m / 10m / 15m / 30m" buttons at the top change both sides.

NOTES
  - This folder uses the data and settings of the "exp tracker" and
    "mesos tracker" folders next to it, so your history carries over.
    Keep those folders where they are.
  - Don't run this and the separate EXP tracker at the same time (they
    use the same dashboard address).
